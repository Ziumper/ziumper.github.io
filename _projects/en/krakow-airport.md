The official website of Kraków Airport (John Paul II International Airport Kraków-Balice), one of the largest airports in Poland. Besides news and passenger information, the site shows live departure and arrival boards, travel and transport guides, and accessibility options such as font size and contrast settings. It is available in Polish and English and runs on Laravel with Livewire.

I worked on it as a PHP Fullstack Developer at [Web24.com.pl](https://web24.com.pl/), a software house in Gdynia. On this project I:

- Implemented updates and maintained the application.
- Prepared the plan for and took on the upgrade of the whole project to the latest versions of PHP and Laravel, together with all of its dependencies.
