BRITA's German online shop for water filter jugs, water dispensers and filter cartridges. The store runs on Shopware 6 with headless elements.

I worked on this store as a PHP Fullstack Developer at [Strix](https://www.strix.net/en), a Shopware Platinum Partner agency. On this project I:

- Designed a Varnish caching concept for the entire store, covering its headless elements as well, by extending the core Shopware layer, so that pages could be served quickly from the cache across the whole shop.
- Extended the backend and the admin panel, streamlining day-to-day store management and the management of integrations that handle order processing.
- Was responsible for monitoring the store: I tracked down errors, analyzed their causes and fixed them, keeping the shop running smoothly for its customers.
