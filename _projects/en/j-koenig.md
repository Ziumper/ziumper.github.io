Online shop of J. König, a supplier of machines and tools for natural stone, tile and ceramic processing. The store runs on Shopware 6 and serves professional B2B customers, with features such as budget management and product catalogs.

I worked on this store as a PHP Fullstack Developer at [Strix](https://www.strix.net/en), a Shopware Platinum Partner agency. On this project I:

- Optimized the store and improved the performance of the admin panel, making everyday work in the shop's back office faster.
- Implemented plugins that extended the shop's functionality.
- Rewrote and improved the custom system that imported customer orders via SFTP, so that it runs more reliably and is easier to maintain.
