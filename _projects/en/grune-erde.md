Online shop of Grüne Erde, an Austrian brand of sustainable furniture, natural mattresses, home textiles, clothing and natural cosmetics. The store runs on Shopware 6.

I worked on this store as a PHP Fullstack Developer at [Strix](https://www.strix.net/en), a Shopware Platinum Partner agency. On this project I:

- Was responsible for the data import and the Google Shopping feed, which brought the shop's products to Google Shopping.
- Implemented many modern frontend solutions that go beyond the standard Shopware framework.
- Conducted code reviews and tested the application.
